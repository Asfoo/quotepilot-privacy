Privacy Policy

QuotePilot: Quote & Invoice Maker · Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short: QuotePilot has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter (your business details, customers, quotes, invoices, payments and receipts) is stored on your device. We never receive it. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy is saved to your own Google Drive, which we cannot access. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob, which shows ads in the free version, Google Play, which processes Premium subscriptions, and Google Drive, if you choose to use it.

This policy explains what information QuotePilot (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles and why. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information you enter

To create quotes, invoices and receipts, you may enter:

  • your business details (business and owner name, address, phone, email, tax number and logo);
  • your customers’ details (names, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses);
  • the content of your documents (line items, prices, taxes, notes and terms) and the payments you record.

This information is saved in a database inside the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see it. It only leaves your device if you share it (section 6) or turn on Google Drive backup (section 5). You are responsible for having the right to store your customers’ details and share documents with them.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or offer an account or sign-in.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your data.
  • Usage statistics and error logs that the app creates stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • We do not sell your information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example, model, operating system and language);
  • how you interact with ads.

Google handles this information under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (on most phones, Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Premium subscribers do not see ads, and no ads are requested for them.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether you have an active subscription, and stores that status on your device. Google’s handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store.

5. Google Drive backup (optional, Premium)

Premium subscribers can connect their Google account to back up their data to Google Drive. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google and grant the app permission to use its own private folder in your Drive (the “app data” folder). The app cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • A backup file with all your app data (the same contents as described in section 1, including your logo) is uploaded to that folder when you connect, when you tap “Back up now”, and automatically about once a day when you open the app. The 10 most recent backups are kept and older ones are deleted.
  • Your Google account’s email address is stored on your device so the app can show which account is connected. It is not sent to us.
  • The backups are stored in your Google account, under Google’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to your Google account or your backups.

You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → Backup & restore), which stops backups and revokes the app’s access. Backups already in your Drive are kept until you delete them: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find QuotePilot and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app’s access from your Google Account.

6. Information you choose to share

Some features send information out of the app, but only when you start them:

  • Sharing or saving PDFs: the document is passed to the app you pick (for example, email, a messaging app or your file storage).
  • Calling, emailing or texting a customer: the app opens your phone, email or messaging app with the customer’s details filled in.
  • Backups: a backup file of your data is created and saved or shared wherever you choose. Anyone with access to that file can read its contents, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Business logo: if you add a logo, you pick one image from your device, and only that image is copied into the app.

Once information leaves the app this way, the app you shared it with is responsible for how it handles it.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual customers and documents in the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app. Backup files you created and backups in your Google Drive are not removed automatically; delete them from wherever you saved them, or from Google Drive as described in section 5. Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For information held by Google, use Google’s tools described in sections 3 to 5.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access on a normal, unmodified device. Protect your device with a screen lock, and store backup files securely. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

QuotePilot is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because the information you enter stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it directly in the app. For information processed by Google for ads or purchases, you can exercise your rights through Google.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
